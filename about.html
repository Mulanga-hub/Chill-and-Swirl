About Chill & Swirl

Welcome to Chill & Swirl Ice Cream, a South African ice cream business created to provide affordable, delicious and exciting frozen treats to our customers.

Chill & Swirl was founded in 2025 with the goal of creating an enjoyable ice cream experience for students, workers, families and the wider community.

Our Story

Chill & Swirl began with the idea of creating a modern and welcoming ice cream store where customers could enjoy traditional favourites as well as creative ice cream combinations.

Our first store is located in Pretoria Central, with a second location in Sunnyside. As the business grows, our goal is to expand to other parts of Pretoria, other provinces and eventually international markets.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, high-quality ice cream and excellent customer service. We aim to build positive relationships between our customers and our team while making delicious treats accessible to the community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow Chill & Swirl from a local Pretoria business into a recognised South African ice cream brand and, in the future, expand internationally.
